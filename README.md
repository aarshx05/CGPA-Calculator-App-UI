# 🎓 CGPA Calculator App with Real-Time Leaderboard

Calculate your CGPA effortlessly and compete with your peers in real-time. The **CGPA Calculator App** offers a sleek, intuitive way for students to track academic performance, set goals, and stay motivated through a competitive leaderboard system.

## 📱 Features

- **📊 GPA Calculation**  
  Simply input your grades and credit hours to calculate your CGPA with precision. The app supports various grading systems for ultimate flexibility.
  
- **🏆 Real-Time Leaderboard**  
  Create your profile and see how you rank globally! Track your CGPA alongside peers to foster healthy academic competition and motivation.

- **🎨 User-Friendly Interface**  
  Designed with simplicity in mind, the clean and intuitive interface ensures seamless navigation, making CGPA tracking a breeze.
